# Swiggy Restaurant Data Analysis (EDA)

## Project Overview
This project explores restaurant listings from Swiggy using Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) in Python. The analysis focuses on restaurant ratings, food categories, pricing, delivery times, and differences across cities.

## Objectives
- Understand the structure and quality of the dataset.
- Explore restaurant ratings, prices, and delivery times.
- Identify the most common food categories.
- Examine relationships between restaurant prices and ratings.
- Compare restaurant characteristics across cities.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Dataset
The dataset contains **8,680 restaurant listings** across **9 cities** and includes information such as:
- City and area
- Restaurant name
- Price
- Average rating
- Total ratings
- Food type
- Address
- Delivery time

## Analysis Performed
1. Data inspection and cleaning
2. Descriptive statistics
3. Univariate analysis of ratings, prices, food categories, and delivery times
4. Bivariate analysis of price and ratings
5. City-wise comparison of restaurant count, average rating, average price, and delivery time
6. Correlation analysis of numerical variables

## Key Findings
- Ratings commonly fall between 3.5 and 4.5, with concentrations around 3.0 and 4.0–4.5.
- Indian cuisine is the most frequently listed food type, followed by Chinese and North Indian.
- Most restaurants fall within the ₹200–₹400 price range.
- Delivery times are commonly around 40–60 minutes.
- Higher prices do not consistently correspond to higher ratings.
- Kolkata has the most restaurant listings, while Chennai has the highest average rating.
- Mumbai has the highest average price, and Ahmedabad has the shortest average delivery time.
